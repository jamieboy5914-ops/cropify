About Our Crop Disease Detection App

Welcome to our Crop Disease Detection App! This web application is designed to help farmers identify crop diseases early, enabling them to take timely action to protect their crops and improve yields.

Features

  • Upload images of crops for disease detection
  • Classify crops as healthy or diseased
  • Provide recommendations for disease management

How It Works

Our app uses a Deep learning model trained on a dataset of images depicting healthy crops and various diseased crops. When you upload an image, the model analyzes it and classifies the crop as healthy or diseased based on visual characteristics.
