About Cropify

Cropify helps identify cotton leaf diseases and pests early, so growers can act in time to protect the crop and the yield.

Features

  • Upload a cotton leaf image for analysis
  • Classify the leaf into one of seven conditions
  • Receive guidance for managing each condition

How it works

A convolutional neural network based on MobileNetV2 was trained on the SAR-CLD-2024 cotton leaf dataset. When you upload an image, the model analyses its visual features and returns the most likely class with treatment guidance.
